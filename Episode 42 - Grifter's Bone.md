Statement of [[Jennifer Ling]], regarding a live musical performance she attended in Soho.

**Date of Statement:** [[November 3, 2013]]

**Case Number:** #0131103

**Points of Interest:**
[[Tommy Moncreef]].
Grifter's Bone is so bad, people rip their ears off.
[[Lee Kipple]] has long blond hair.
> "Lee obviously gets a lot of comments about listening to crappy music, and if it's one of the older folk you can bet they'll mention Grifter's Bone. It took me a while to notice how weird his reaction was when this happened. Whereas most of the staff would fake laugh, sigh or maybe swear a little, Lee would go very still. He'd nod gently, and reach up to scratch his ears, still mostly covered by hair. No-one seemed to notice."

[[Lee Kipple]] covers his ears a lot.
A man came on stage with a keyboard.
> "The man was short, so Lee said, and painfully thin, wearing a ratty, old brown suit that draped around him like, in Lee's words, “flaps of ill-fitting skin”. His thinning black hair was slicked back, and his face had a strange look of cruelty to it. As he placed his fingers on his instrument, they left behind dark red spots on the bright white keys. Lee said he had never heard of Grifter's Bone before that moment, but somehow he knew that’s what he was looking at. And then the music started." ([[Long Black Hair]]) ([[Strange Man (Grifter's Bone)]])

[[Lee Kipple]] was wearing earplugs, and around them, was dried up human blood. The doctors couldn't help.
[[Lee Kipple]] made an article about Grifter's Bone, and someone commented, “Tonight. Soho.” and “No earplugs required”.
> "I'd kept my watch maybe an hour when I saw someone staring at me from the doorway of a small shop. The sign above didn't have an obvious name, simply reading “Crystals. Books. Tarot”. He was tall, black and careworn, deep lines of worry etched into an otherwise handsome face."

The man said she should wear something to protect her ears.
The Grifter's Bone players came along.
> "Their features were hard to be sure of in the dark, but walking at the front, dragging a rolling keyboard case, was a short, thin man in an oversized brown suit. The three figures behind him were all much taller than him, but each was stick thin and moved with a jerkiness to their motions." ([[Creature with Long Limbs]])

She followed them into a jazz bar. It was almost empty.
> "Most seemed to be dressed for an evening of jazz, but I noticed a few of them seemed to be wearing thick coats or jackets, and one, an older white man with a shock of silver hair, appeared to be wearing a silk bathrobe."

She set her phone up to record the performance, then left.
> "Then I heard it. Muffled through the walls, but still rising distinctly to where I stood, a note. A single, clear note, from what sounded like a cello. It was joined by others, a keyboard, a guitar, and over them all the pure piping sound of a flute. It was beautiful."
