Statement of [[Mary Keay]], regarding her first encounter with a book that would end up in the library of [[Jurgen Leitner]].

**Date of Statement:** [[July 3, 2008]]

**Case Number:** #0080307

**Points of Interest:**
[[1955]] is when [[Mary Keay]] found the book.
[[Mary Keay]]'s mother worked in the [[Magnus Institute|Institute]].
Dr [[Margaret Tillerson]].
> "She was tall and thin, with long, dark hair pulled into a severe bun. She wore a deep blue woollen dress and carried an old leather briefcase that seemed constantly on the verge of buckling, although she carried it with ease. I don't know what it was exactly about her that stuck out to me, but as soon as I saw her, I knew she was what my mother had always talked about. She was touched by powers like those that watched over our family."

Iron Safe. 24-18-3-15.
[[Strange Girl (First Edition)]]
> "Then, shortly before she was due to close for the day, a short, matronly woman arrived. She had curly brown hair, seemed to be in perfect health and smiled like a fool as she made her way into Dr Tillerson's office."

Two books.
> "Inside, I saw two books, one small and bound in leather, the other large and misshapen. As she retrieved the larger of the two, she brushed away what looked to be a small pile of animal bones and picked up a wickedly sharp looking fountain pen. She leaned over the still form on the table and began to write, not in the book, but on the flesh of the woman she had killed. I could see even from my hiding place that her handwriting was cramped and messy, leaving some of the blue ink flowing off her subject like blood." ([[Bone Book]] [[Large Book (First Edition)]])

> "As she spoke, I felt the air grow thick and heavy, a scent like wet dirt rolling through the building and settling in my chest. I don't know exactly when he appeared. In fact, even now I'm still not able to pinpoint the moment they arrive. Like falling asleep, it has simply happened already."

Man appeared out of nowhere from the [[Large Book (First Edition)]].
[[Mary Keay]] stabbed [[Margaret Tillerson]] in the throat.
[[Mary Keay]] now has the [[Bone Book]].
[[Bone Book]].
> "Poems about dying animals, also in Sanskrit. Drops a lot of bones. I, I don't even think it has a real title. Pointless really. I eventually sold it to Leitner, though it came back to me after the attack."
