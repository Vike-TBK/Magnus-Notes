Dr [[Margaret Tillerson]].

She wore a deep blue woollen dress and carried an old leather briefcase that seemed constantly on the verge of buckling, although she carried it with ease. I don't know what it was exactly about her that stuck out to me, but as soon as I saw her, I knew she was what my mother had always talked about. She was touched by powers like those that watched over our family."

Two books.
> "Inside, I saw two books, one small and bound in leather, the other large and misshapen. As she retrieved the larger of the two, she brushed away what looked to be a small pile of animal bones and picked up a wickedly sharp looking fountain pen. I could see even from my hiding place that her handwriting was cramped and messy, leaving some of the blue ink flowing off her subject like blood." ([[Bone Book]] [[Large Book (First Edition)]])
