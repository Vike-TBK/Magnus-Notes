Statement of [[Yuri Utkin]], regarding an encounter with a strange circus the village of Algasovo, central Russia.

**Date of Statement:** [[March 2, 1979]]
Recorded by [[Gertrude Robinson]] on [[April 15, 1997]]

**Case Number:** #9790302

**Points of Interest:**
[[Gertrude Robinson|Gertrude]]'s statement formating.
> Case 9790302. [[Yuri Utkin]]. Incident occurred in the village of Algasovo, central Russia, November 1952. Statement given 2nd of March 1979. Committed to tape 15th of April 1997.
> [[Gertrude Robinson]] recording.

[[Ivan Utkin]].
[[Piotr]].
Over the entrance stood a brightly painted wooden sign that read “Другой Цирк”, “Another Circus”.
There's a [[Calliope]] in the circus.
Circus member sounds robotic.
Robot Tiger.
> " It seemed to shift its position slowly, like a doll having its joints twisted, but its face remained completely still. The mouth stayed curled to reveal its teeth, the ears stayed alert and pointed forward, and the eyes still stared out, though where they had at first seemed brilliant, they now had an almost glassy look to them. Without warning it roared, the same powerful cry of violence that I had heard before, but as it did so I fell back in surprise. The tiger’s mouth had not moved."

[[Two Mysterious Men]] make an appearance.
> "As I scrambled back, I felt a large hand on my shoulder, and looked up to see two huge men in overalls. They lifted me easily, so my feet hung almost two feet from the ground. They talked fast, crude Russian, and their words seemed to shift back and forth between them."

A tent with a sign written in English, "Freak Show."
> "A mouth cannot function if it’s located anywhere other than the face. Limbs cannot bend like rubber. A man cannot walk and talk and stare without a head. You will, I hope, forgive my lack of precise descriptions. It has been 27 years since that night, and I can no longer clearly distinguish between what is memory and what is nightmare."

Weird bag.
> "It contorted itself, pulsing and throbbing like a wounded animal’s stomach, and fell heavily forward."

Clowns were fighting violently in the main tent.
> "One of them, huge and scowling in white and purple polka-dots, pinned down its smaller companion, whose bright yellow shirt was now streaked with red. With each blow from the big clown, the crowd, among whom I could clearly see my father, howled with laughter and cheers." (Sounds similar to the [[Clown Doll]] from [[Episode 24 - Strange Music]])

[[Ivan Utkin]] was on the circus tightrope. He didn't fall.
> "I do not know how long I had stood there watching, but it seemed like only a moment later I felt my father’s hand grip me by the shoulder. I turned to see him standing there with Ivan by his side. He had a look on his face as though he had eaten something that had spoiled, and without a word he led us out of the circus and back to our home. The field was empty by the next morning."

[[Ivan Utkin]] doesn't remember what happened, it was a blur to him. But he still gets nightmares from it.
> "Every November, around when the circus had come to Algasovo, he would dream that he was there again. He could smell the sawdust and hear the steam-organ playing, but he could not move. In the dream he would find himself tightly bound with coarse rope and trapped inside a thick hessian sack. I remembered those nights. He always woke up screaming."
